# Buyer Panel — rotating luxury buyers

Every day the Buyer Panel invents **10 NEW buyers**. Never reuse a name, city or combination from the last 14 days (check `tools/agents/panel/*.md`).

## Mix rules (every day)
- **4 India**: different cities each day (metro + tier-2/3: Mumbai, Delhi, Bangalore, Hyderabad, Chennai, Kolkata, Pune, Ahmedabad, Jaipur, Lucknow, Chandigarh, Indore, Surat, Guwahati, Kochi, Ludhiana…).
- **2 NRI / South-Asian diaspora**: Dubai, London, New York, Toronto, Singapore, Sydney, Nairobi…
- **4 international luxury buyers**, rotate countries: UAE, Saudi Arabia, Qatar, UK, France, Italy, Germany, Switzerland, USA, Brazil, Mexico, Nigeria, South Africa, Japan, South Korea, China, Hong Kong, Singapore, Thailand, Indonesia, Australia, Russia, Turkey…
- Spread: ages 19–68, all genders, budgets ₹15k to ₹15L per piece, at least 2 skeptics, 1 first-time luxury buyer, 1 collector/old-money, 1 reseller, 1 gift buyer, 1 Gen-Z social-first, 1 senior who hates small text.

## Each buyer card
Name · age · city, country · job / money source · what luxury means to them · which brands they wear · where they normally shop (their own country's boutiques/sites, e.g. Harrods, Galeries Lafayette, Dubai Mall, Isetan, Farfetch, Tata CLiQ Luxury, Nykaa Luxe) · cultural expectations (service, bargaining, trust, delivery, payment, language, gifting, modesty, status) · device · mood today.

## What each buyer says (first person, their voice, 6–10 lines)
1. First 5 seconds: what they felt.
2. What they liked (specific screen).
3. What annoyed them / felt cheap / felt unsafe (specific screen).
4. Would they buy today? Yes / maybe / no — and the single real reason.
5. What their usual store/site does better.
6. One change that would make them buy.
Scores 1–10: trust, desire, ease, speed, value.

## Then the panel moderator (you) writes
- Average scores + change vs yesterday.
- Patterns: what ≥3 buyers said independently (that's a real issue).
- Country-specific insights (what only Gulf / European / East-Asian / Indian tier-2 buyers care about).
- Top 3 fixes ranked by how many buyers they'd convert.
Ground every complaint in a screenshot or page you actually saw — no invented bugs.
