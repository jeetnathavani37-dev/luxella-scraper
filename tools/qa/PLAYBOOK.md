## Buyer personas (used by the Buyer Panel)
- **Riya, 29, Mumbai** — Coach/Kate Spade every month, impatient, mobile-only, Instagram-native.
- **Arjun, 38, Delhi** — gifts for his wife; needs trust, clear price, a delivery date, easy WhatsApp.
- **Meera, 45, Bangalore** — shops LV/Gucci/Dior boutiques; judges taste, typography and polish harshly.
- **Kabir, 24, Indore** — reseller; speed, catalogue depth, sharing pieces to his own customers.
Score each 1–10 on trust, desire, ease, speed; say why they would buy, why they would not, and what a competitor (louisvuitton.com/in, gucci.com/in, farfetch.com/in, luxury.tatacliq.com, net-a-porter.com) does better on the same screen.
