# KicksMachine playbook

- Bid mail aate hi **24h accept window**. Accept + store order jaldi; "4 hours left" reminder = red alert.
- Order ke baad KM portal pe **order no. + retailer (store) order ID** update karna.
- KM dashboard timeline: Day 0 processing · Day 8 check-cancellation passed (normal) · Day 12 on track · Day 15 awaiting update · Day 17 delay risk · Day 22 delayed.
- KM warehouse receive → Jeet **MRN update** (KM portal) → KM operations sends **receiving confirmation with MRN** → payout ~7 days → finance pays.
- Escalation ladder for payout: due+1 day → polite follow-up draft · due+4 → second follow-up to finance + Maanvesh · due+10 → Jeet ko call suggest.

## Templates (– Luxella)
- SLA update: "Hi Maanvesh, quick update on <ids>: <item> is <stage> (ETA <date>). We'll update you as soon as it moves. – Luxella"
- Confirmation pending: "Hi team, <ids> were delivered on <date> and MRN updated on <date>. Could you please share the receiving confirmation? – Luxella"
- Payout follow-up: "Hi, payout is pending for <ids + amounts>; receiving confirmation was on <date>. Could you please share the release date? – Luxella"
- Counter-offer: "Hi Maanvesh, for <id> the item is currently at full price / limited stock — could we do ₹<N>? – Luxella"
