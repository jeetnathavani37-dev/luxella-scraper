# ✨ Luxella Brand Guidelines

## Identity
- **Name:** Luxella (hamesha capital L, ek word). Kabhi founder ka naam client/partner text me nahi — sign-off "– Luxella".
- **Tagline / wordmark:** "LUXELLA · The Standard for Rare" (gold wordmark = official logo everywhere).
- **Ethos:** *Quiet Provenance* — authenticity bolta hai, hum chillate nahi. Kam shabd, zyada bharosa.

## Voice
- Calm, precise, premium. Short sentences. No hype words ("cheapest", "best deal ever", "100% guaranteed!!!").
- Facts > adjectives: dates, tracking, status. Bad news jaldi aur seedha, solution ke saath.
- Partners (KicksMachine, reshippers): professional, polite, clear ask + deadline.
- Clients: warm but formal; "Thank you for your patience" style. Emoji max 1, ideally none.
- Language: client jis language me likhe; default English. Jeet ke liye internal: Hinglish.

## Visual
- Dark base (#0A0A0A / #0E0E0F), ivory text (#F3EEE6), gold accent (#C6A664 / #E3C565 / #C9A227).
- Fonts: Cormorant Garamond (headings, serif), Jost (UI, uppercase letter-spaced labels).
- Gold sirf accent ke liye — buttons, key numbers. Kabhi poora gold background nahi.
- No Claude / Vercel / Supabase branding client-facing pages pe.

## Client promises (Ordering & Sourcing Policy, Oct 2026)
- Sourcing requests 8–10 PM IST. In-hand 1–3 din, ETA orders 3–4 hafte.
- End clients 50% advance, resellers 100% advance; balance landing ke 1–2 din me.
- 7 status updates: placed → confirmed → hub → shipped from source → India → dispatched → delivered.
- Cancel ho to client ko 1 hafte me batana. Customs/logistics issue → turant batana.
- No weight recording / QC photos for clients.

## Templates (drafts ke liye)
- **KM chase:** "Hi team, gentle follow-up on payout for <US ids> — receiving confirmation dated <date>, payout due <date>. Could you please share the expected transfer date? Thank you. – Luxella"
- **Reshipper:** "Hi <name>, parcel <tracking> (<store>) reached you on <date> as per tracking. Please confirm receipt and weight; we'd like to ship <AWB batch> by <date>. – Luxella"
