# Pricing playbook (bid → GO/NO)

**Landed (INR) = price × (1 − discount) × (1 + tax) × FX + reship + ₹150 domestic**

- FX: USD 95.5 · CAD 70 · GBP 129 (card markup included).
- Discount: Michael Kors new-account first order 10%. Sale price dikh raha ho to wahi lo (discount 0). **Coupon codes (EXTRA15 etc.) count nahi.**
- Tax: 0 via Delaware (Ishaan/Yash). Prabhmeet NJ = 7%.
- Reship: billed kg × ₹1,250. Ishaan: **1 kg minimum, 0.5 kg round-up** → stack karke 1 kg+ banao. Yash: actual weight.
- Weight guide (kg): wallet/belt/cap 0.3 · small crossbody 0.6 · medium bag 0.9 · large tote/carryall 1.2 · sandals/sneakers 1.2 · boots 1.8 · clothing 0.5.
- Margin % = (payout − landed) / landed × 100.
- Verdict: **GO ≥ 20** · **TIGHT 12–20** · **NO < 12** or out of stock · **CHECK** price confirm nahi hua.
