Your client page, explained

A short guide to everything on your page. Takes 3 minutes. Keep it handy.

1. Getting started

  1. Open the link Luxella sent you and sign in with your email and password.
  2. Add it to your phone's home screen so it opens like an app (browser menu, then "Add to Home Screen").
  3. When asked, allow notifications. You will get an alert whenever your order moves, a payment is recorded, or Luxella replies.
Choose which alerts you want in Account, under notifications.

3. The 9 order stages

  1. Order placed: we have your request.
  2. Order confirmed: bought in your name.
  3. Shipped by retailer/outlet: on its way to our hub.
  4. Received at international hub: arrived and being checked.
  5. Shipped from international hub: left for India.
  6. Received at Indian warehouse: landed, clearing and getting ready.
  7. Address requested: we need your delivery address. Open the Address tab.
  8. Shipped from Indian warehouse: out for delivery. Please record an unboxing video.
  9. Delivered: done. Thank you.
"ETA" orders are imported and have an expected date. "In hand (Local)" orders are already in India and move faster.

4. Payments

  1. Open Payments and check the amount due and the payment details.
  2. Pay as instructed, then submit your payment reference or screenshot.
  3. Status: Submitted, then Verified by Luxella (you get an alert). If it is Rejected, the reason is shown. Fix it and submit again.
Always attach the reference number. It makes verification quick.

5. Delivery address

When an order reaches stage 7, you get an alert. Open Address, pick a saved address or add a new one, check the pin code and phone number, and send it. You can save addresses in advance to be faster.

6. Help, chat and messages

Ask Luxella (chat bubble)

Instant answers about your orders, dues, tracking and policies.

Message Luxella

In the same chat bubble, tap "Message Luxella" to write to us directly. You can add photos and record a voice note. We reply in the same place and you get an alert.

Support cases

For a problem with an order, open Help, choose the issue, add photos and send. You can follow the status until it is resolved.

WhatsApp

For anything urgent, use the WhatsApp button. Hours: 11AM to 10PM.

7. Quick answers

Where is my order?

Tracker tab. The highlighted stage is where it is now.

How do I know my payment is accepted?

The status turns Verified and you get an alert.

Why am I not getting alerts?

Allow notifications for the page in your phone settings and open the page once after adding it to your home screen.

Can I change my address after sending it?

Message Luxella as early as possible, before the parcel ships from India.

What should I do on delivery?

Record a continuous unboxing video before opening. It protects you if anything is wrong.
